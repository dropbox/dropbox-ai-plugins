# Office Router

## Route map

| Signal | Office |
| --- | --- |
| Email draft, send, calendar, alarm, reminder | Mail & Schedule |
| Rent, debt, collectors, credit, bank, payoff | Money & Debt |
| VA claim, CUE, HLR, TDIU, DBQ, VSO, HUD-VASH, SCCHA | VA Benefits |
| File, Dropbox, Drive, archive, quarantine, security check | File Vault |
| School, Mission College, IBEW, ETASV, KAAVE hours, job | School & Work |
| Photo, album, screenshot catalog, design | Photo Design & Creation |
| Cross-office conflict (>3 handoffs) | Mission Control → Tam |

## Rules

1. One owner per task on the Team Task Board.
2. Do not do another office's deep work from Mission Control.
3. After 3 back-and-forths between offices, escalate to Tam with both positions.
4. Outbound messages stay **Draft ready** until Tam says send.
