# CP / DBQ Prep Card

## Produce a 1-page pocket card

1. **30-second opening statement**
2. **DBQ symptoms to report** (only verified)
3. **Do-not-discuss list** (other piles, OPEN cites, unrelated claims)

## Rules

- Never invent exam sites or dates.
- Keep other legal piles off this card.
- DRAFT for Tam; Mail & Schedule only places calendar reminders.
